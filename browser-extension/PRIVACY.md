# Xiletrade Sync – Privacy policy

Xiletrade Sync is a browser extension that sends your Path of Exile session cookie to the Xiletrade desktop application installed on your computer.

## Data accessed
- The session cookie of pathofexile.com, poe.kakaogames.com, pathofexile.tw and poe.game.qq.com, read only when you open the extension popup or click **Send**.

## Data use
- The cookie is passed to the Xiletrade application on the same computer through the `xiletrade://` link, only after your click. The application stores it encrypted on your device to authenticate its requests to the official trade site.
- The extension does not store the cookie and does not send it, or anything else, to any server, the developer included.
- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no remote code.

## Data sharing
- None. No data is sold or transferred to third parties.

## Removal
- Uninstalling the extension removes it entirely. In Xiletrade, the saved session can be removed at any time (Settings > Developer manager > Remove token).
